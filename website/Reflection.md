### Reflection (Word Count: ~321 words)

In this assignment, I worked with Generative AI to design and build my personal academic website. My goal was to share my professional journey: moving from over four years in Financial and IT Auditing at Grant Thornton Vietnam to pursuing my Master’s in Information Management at Chang Gung University. I used the AI to help structure the website layout, feature my research on corporate bankruptcy prediction, and clearly map out my career evolution from financial audit to IT audit and ultimately AI assurance.

The AI proved to be a fast, helpful tool for technical setup. It quickly created the clean HTML5 and Tailwind CSS layout, and wrote the JavaScript for interactive features like dark mode, project filters, and popup modals. It also made my complex research topic much easier to present by breaking down my multi-phase methodology into clear visual sections. Most importantly, it helped me keep the website simple and lightweight, allowing me to deploy it directly to GitHub Pages without any complex setup.

However, the collaboration also showed clear limitations. At first, the text written by the AI was full of generic buzzwords and missed the practical nuances between financial accounting standards and IT controls. On the technical side, it made simple mistakes, such as generating code that broke on Windows file names and modals that did not scroll properly on mobile screens. I stepped in to fix the code, optimize the responsive design for phones, and personally rewrite the story of my career transition to reflect my real experiences and thoughts.

Ultimately, this project proved that Generative AI is a fantastic prototyping assistant, but it cannot work well on its own. While it can generate layouts and code in seconds, it lacks personal voice and practical context. The real success of the project came from using my own domain experience and critical judgment to guide the AI, turning raw generated code into an authentic and meaningful personal portfolio.
