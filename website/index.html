Hi! I'm Võ Bích Loan

I started my career with several years as a financial auditor, working with diverse businesses to evaluate their accounting and financial health. In auditing, because the volume of transactions is massive, testing is limited to samples rather than the entire dataset. This made me realize that to truly verify data, I needed to understand the IT systems that create it in the first place. This led me to transfer to the IT audit department for about a year, and eventually move to Taiwan with an academic scholarship to pursue a Master’s in Information Management at Chang Gung University. During my studies, I focused on Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, which naturally brought me to AI Assurance. As more companies integrate AI into their core operations, I believe auditing these models is essential to assess their fairness, accuracy, and risk. By connecting my audit background with my technical skills in IT and ML, my goal is to help businesses evaluate and trust the AI systems they deploy.
